一、一分钟内理解整个项目

我们用葡萄牙两家酒店的历史预订记录，研究哪些订单最终取消或未到店。模型输入是预订特征，输出是风险概率。先用早期数据学习，再在晚期数据上评价。逻辑回归比人人相同风险的baseline略好，但默认报警漏掉大多数实际取消或未到店的订单。数据是较晚的快照，筛选又改变了人群，所以现有结果不能证明创建预订时可以可靠上线。

English overview:
We use historical records from two Portuguese hotels to estimate the risk of cancellation or no-show. Logistic regression provides modest ranking and probability improvement over a constant baseline. However, the default alert rule misses most cases. Later snapshots and cohort selection limit what these results can support.

第4页：What do we predict? X, Y and a probability

使用安排：45秒；建议Zhe Wang讲

英文讲稿：
Y is the eventual recorded outcome. One includes cancellation or no-show; zero means a completed stay recorded as Check-Out. Lead time means days between booking and arrival. X includes the hotel, lead time, planned stay, guest counts and booking choices. Logistic regression returns a probability. A value of 0.8 would mean an estimated 80 percent risk. This example explains the output. Our primary loss is log loss, which checks probabilities against outcomes and penalizes confident mistakes. Lower is better. 中文理解：X是模型用来判断的记录信息，Y是最终结果：1=取消或未到店，0=完成入住。模型输出概率p；阈值是之后把概率转成报警的规则。Log loss对概率和结果进行评分，特别惩罚自信但错误的预测。

重点与边界：p=0.8是概率含义的说明，不是我们模型已校准的证据；不能说所有给0.8的订单实际一定80%取消。

第8页：Analysis: a constant baseline and logistic regression

使用安排：50秒；建议Zhe Wang讲

英文讲稿：
Our baseline assigns every booking the training positive fraction, 30.75 percent. It uses no individual details and no validation outcomes. Logistic regression combines booking inputs into log-odds, then converts them to a probability. Regularization discourages extreme coefficients. We fit once on training, with fixed settings and no parameter search. Both models face exactly the same validation rows. We evaluate probabilities with log loss and ordering with AUC. AUC asks how often a positive receives a higher score than a negative. A constant baseline has AUC 0.5.

中文理解：Baseline给所有订单同一个概率，即训练正类比例30.75%。它不区分个别订单。Logistic regression根据输入计算不同概率。两者必须在同一批验证记录上比较，才能公平回答模型是否增加信息。

重点与边界：逻辑回归先计算一个加权分数，再通过sigmoid转换为0到1的概率。L2限制极端权重；它有助于约束模型，但不会自动消除偏差或泄漏。

三、详细问答：中文理解与英文短答

先自己回答，再对照。优先练Q5–Q11（时点与筛选）、Q15–Q21（指标与阈值）、Q27–Q28（bootstrap）。

Q1. 为什么选酒店取消预测？

Professor may ask: Why did you choose this question?

English answer: We chose an applied prediction problem that connects booking information with service planning. It also lets us examine how information timing and evaluation choices affect a model’s credibility.

中文解释与边界：题目有业务背景，也能展示课程里的预测、基线和评价设计。可以表达你们现在的兴趣，不能编造酒店工作经历或项目收益。

Q2. 你们预测的是取消，还是取消加未到店？

Professor may ask: What exactly is your target?

English answer: Our target preserves is_canceled. A positive outcome includes cancellation or no-show, and a negative outcome is a recorded completed stay. We use the term noncompletion to make this clear.

中文解释与边界：标签不是单纯主动取消；no-show也为1。目标是最终记录结果，不是未来30天会不会取消。

Q3. 一行数据代表一个人吗？

Professor may ask: What does one row represent?

English answer: One row is a booking. A person can make several bookings, and a group can produce multiple similar records. We cannot assume that all rows are independent guests.

中文解释与边界：119,390是订单记录数。没有guest/group ID，无法证明所有行独立。重复对模型权重和不确定性都有影响。

Q4. 119,390行为什么只训练39,865行？

Professor may ask: Why do you train on only part of the raw data?

English answer: The raw file spans multiple periods. We separate development and later evaluation, exclude the left boundary and apply outcome-date and departure eligibility rules. Our performance applies to the included cohorts.

中文解释与边界：原始数据总数与实际训练集是不同概念。六类完整计数：训练39,865；训练排除17,610；验证14,088；验证排除12,484；原holdout26,565；左边界8,778。加起来119,390。

Q5. 实际预测时点是什么？

Professor may ask: When would the prediction be made?

English answer: The intended business time is reservation creation. Our current experiment uses retrospective recorded attributes, so it does not establish performance at that original time.

中文解释与边界：先区分希望实现的业务与实际可验证实验。不能只说“创建时预测”，遗漏数据无法验证的限制。

Q6. 什么是数据泄漏？为什么删字段还不够？

Professor may ask: Why is removing obvious leakage insufficient?

English answer: Final status reveals the answer, so it cannot be an input. We remove obvious later information, but the remaining attributes may have changed since creation. Deleting columns does not recover the original values.

中文解释与边界：相对于预测时点定义泄漏。最后房间或累计修改可能在以后才知道。输入像预订特征也不意味着取值是最初版本。

Q7. 用了status date筛选算不算偷偷给模型答案？

Professor may ask: Why use status date for eligibility?

English answer: Status date helps define which outcomes are recorded by the cutoff. It never enters the model. However, selecting on resolution timing can change the outcome mix, so we disclose this selection limitation.

中文解释与边界：模型直接输入与样本筛选是两种作用。没放进X，不代表它对评价没影响。筛选仍可能导致选择偏差。

Q8. 为什么不是随机切分？

Professor may ask: Why use time-based evaluation?

English answer: We want to learn from earlier records and evaluate later ones. A random split mixes periods. Our time ordering is based on a derived date, so it remains an approximate retrospective design.

中文解释与边界：时间划分更接近未来记录评价，但日期是proxy；不要声称它完全模拟真实线上系统。

Q9. booking date怎么算？

Professor may ask: How do you estimate booking date?

English answer: We subtract lead time from recorded arrival date. This gives a proxy rather than an independently verified immutable creation timestamp.

中文解释与边界：例如10月20日到店、提前10天：推算10月10日。原release缺少独立创建时间来核对。

Q10. 为什么已取消的订单还要等计划离店？

Professor may ask: Why require planned departure for canceled bookings?

English answer: A canceled booking can already have a known outcome. The extra departure gate limits preferential admission of early cancellations for future stays, but it also changes the cohort. It is a design restriction, not necessary label latency.

中文解释与边界：不能回答“因为标签还没成熟”：有些标签已经知道。正确解释是选定人群的规则，并承认代价。

Q11. 你们的成熟规则无偏吗？

Professor may ask: Is the maturity rule unbiased?

English answer: No. About 47 percent of validation candidates are excluded. A status-only rule would add 4,080 positives and change prevalence from 26.19 to 42.76 percent. Neither rule automatically gives an unbiased cohort.

中文解释与边界：这是一项大限制，不能用“conservative”一词当保证。性能只对筛选后人群成立。

Q12. 为什么用训练比例当baseline？

Professor may ask: Why use training prevalence as the baseline?

English answer: It is a transparent minimum benchmark that gives every booking the same probability without using validation outcomes. The model must add value beyond that simple rule on the same evaluation rows.

中文解释与边界：0.30749来自训练，而非验证。它没有个体排序能力。这个baseline合理，但战胜它不能证明已战胜所有合理模型。

Q13. 逻辑回归到底在做什么？

Professor may ask: How does logistic regression work here?

English answer: It forms a weighted score from the input columns and converts that score to a probability with the logistic function. L2 regularization discourages extreme weights.

中文解释与边界：可理解为z=截距+各输入乘权重，p=1/(1+exp(-z))。模型对处理后的输入在log-odds上是线性的；不是普通线性回归直接输出任意数值。

Q14. 为什么不用Random Forest？

Professor may ask: Why not use Random Forest?

English answer: Our midterm establishes a defensible initial analysis with a simple fixed model. Timing and cohort issues need to be understood first. Greater model complexity cannot recover missing booking-time information.

中文解释与边界：不是说随机森林不好或一定没帮助。当前教授没有要求复杂模型，先解释清楚现有设计。若后来比较，要在开发阶段设计，不能按已看测试结果反复选模型。

Q15. 什么是log loss？

Professor may ask: What does log loss measure?

English answer: It compares predicted probabilities with observed outcomes and strongly penalizes confident mistakes. Lower is better. It evaluates probabilities rather than a particular alert threshold.

中文解释与边界：单行loss=-[y ln(p)+(1-y)ln(1-p)]，总体取平均。示例：实际y=1，p=0.8的loss约0.223；p=0.1约2.303。这些是公式示例，不是项目个案。

Q16. AUC=0.6584怎么解释？

Professor may ask: How should we interpret the AUC?

English answer: Within our selected validation cohort, a positive booking is ranked above a negative booking about 65.84 percent of the time, with half credit for ties. This is modest ranking information, not classification accuracy.

中文解释与边界：随机抽一条正例和一条负例比较分数。不能说“模型预测对65.84%的订单”，也不能说AUC证明概率校准。

Q17. accuracy接近74%，为什么报警差？

Professor may ask: Why is high accuracy misleading here?

English answer: Most validation bookings complete. A rule that alerts nobody is already correct on 73.81 percent of rows, but finds no positive cases. Our default model detects only 11 of 3,689 positives.

中文解释与边界：验证负例10,399/14,088=73.81%。多数类准确率可以很高，同时漏掉所有少数类。

Q18. precision和recall区别是什么？

Professor may ask: What is the difference between precision and recall?

English answer: Precision is the fraction of alerts that are actual cases. Recall is the fraction of all actual cases that receive an alert. They use different denominators.

中文解释与边界：0.5时precision=11/(11+24)=31.43%；recall=11/3,689=0.30%。误报FP：真实完成却报警；漏报FN：真实取消/未到店却不报警。

Q19. 为什么阈值降低？什么时候可以选0.3？

Professor may ask: Could you choose a 0.3 threshold?

English answer: It finds more cases, but also creates 1,398 false alerts in validation. We report it as a diagnostic. Choosing a practical threshold requires review capacity and the relative costs of missed cases and false alerts.

中文解释与边界：0.3找到1,058例，recall28.68%。不知道每次人工复核成本、误报损害、漏报代价，就不能说它最优。

Q20. 降阈值precision不是应该下降吗，为什么这里升了？

Professor may ask: Why is precision higher at 0.3 than at 0.5?

English answer: Precision does not have to change monotonically with the threshold. At 0.5 we have only 35 alerts, of which 11 are cases. At 0.3 we have 2,456 alerts, of which 1,058 are cases. The selected groups have different case fractions.

中文解释与边界：固定分数降低阈值会增加或维持检出与误报数量，但precision是比例，不一定单调。尤其0.5报警组很小，不能机械套用“降低阈值precision一定降低”。

Q21. 最高分10%到底意味着什么？

Professor may ask: What does the top-ten-percent result show?

English answer: We select 1,409 highest-scored validation bookings. Of them, 679 are actual cases, giving 48.19 percent precision. They contain only 18.41 percent of all cases. It is a retrospective concentration check, not a tested daily workflow.

中文解释与边界：10%是订单选择比例，48.19%是选中组正例比例，18.41%是全部正例中被选到的比例。不要把三者混起来。

Q22. 哪些订单容易误判？

Professor may ask: What does the error analysis show?

English answer: At the default threshold, the few alerts tend to involve longer lead times. The median is 125 days for the 11 true positives and 157 days for the 24 false positives, compared with 37 days for false negatives. These small groups suggest a pattern to investigate.

中文解释与边界：只是描述结果，不能推断“提前预订导致取消”。检出和误报组很小，需避免把中位数当稳定规律。

Q23. 为什么不直接删除重复记录？

Professor may ask: Why keep identical-looking rows?

English answer: Without booking identifiers, identical values can represent different group reservations. We retain them in the primary analysis and check training deduplication separately from unique-row evaluation.

中文解释与边界：训练去重减少重复样本对拟合的权重；验证去重改变评分对象。两种实验回答不同问题，不能混作同一模型对比。

Q24. 缺失儿童人数为什么不是填0？

Professor may ask: Why distinguish missing children from zero?

English answer: Zero is an observed count. Missing means the count is unknown. We use the training median and keep a missingness indicator, so the preparation rule does not treat unknown as an observed zero.

中文解释与边界：训练中位数即使是0，也不等于认为缺失原本就是0。agent/company的NULL是不适用；这些字段没用于模型。不能仅凭缺失表推断MCAR/MAR/MNAR。

Q25. One-hot和standardization是什么？

Professor may ask: Why encode categories and scale numbers?

English answer: Indicator columns represent categories numerically. Scaling puts numeric inputs on a comparable scale, which supports regularized logistic fitting. All learned values come from training.

中文解释与边界：例如酒店名字转成指示列。标准化大致是(x-训练均值)/训练标准差。不能先在所有数据上学习这些统计量再切分。

Q26. 为什么月份要用sin/cos？

Professor may ask: Why use sine and cosine for month?

English answer: Months form a cycle. December and January are neighbors, so fixed sine and cosine terms represent that circular relationship. They do not use validation labels.

中文解释与边界：直接1到12让12与1数值距离很大。编码为sin(2πm/12)、cos(2πm/12)表达循环。它是一项建模假设，不保证捕捉所有季节变化。

Q27. bootstrap怎么做，为什么按周？

Professor may ask: Why resample booking weeks?

English answer: We resample 27 weeks with replacement 400 times, using the same sample for the model and baseline. Keeping rows from a selected week together preserves some within-week dependence. The fitted model stays fixed.

中文解释与边界：不是重新生成400个训练模型。一轮抽27个周，周可以重复或缺席；每个被选周的订单一起进入评分。仍依赖周可交换等假设。

Q28. 那个95%范围是否说明模型一定稳定？

Professor may ask: What does the bootstrap interval guarantee?

English answer: It does not guarantee population coverage. It summarizes conditional variation under the chosen resampling assumptions and fixed fitted model. Training uncertainty, unknown guest links and timing or selection bias remain.

中文解释与边界：0.6376–0.6822是重采样AUC的中间95%范围。不能翻译成“未来AUC有95%概率一定在这里”。

Q29. 城市酒店取消率更高，是酒店类型造成的吗？

Professor may ask: Does hotel type cause the difference?

English answer: We observe a higher training noncompletion fraction at the city hotel. There is only one establishment of each type, and customer mix or cohort selection can differ. This does not identify a causal hotel-type effect.

中文解释与边界：城市35.05%、度假22.35%是相关性描述。酒店类型、酒店身份和客源等因素混在一起，不能由两家酒店得出类型因果结论。

Q30. 有没有显著打败baseline？

Professor may ask: Is the improvement statistically significant?

English answer: The observed validation scores improve over the constant baseline. We also provide a conditional week-bootstrap stability check. We do not present it as a general population significance guarantee.

中文解释与边界：不要随口说“统计显著”。报告范围是观察到的指标改善与有限稳定性诊断，未知依赖、选择与测量限制仍在。

Q31. 2017年test表现如何？之后还能用吗？

Professor may ask: What happened on the later test?

English answer: The unchanged model had AUC 0.6644 and log loss 0.5968, compared with baseline log loss 0.6328. Recall at 0.5 was 6.62 percent. We have seen these results, so later tuning requires genuinely new evaluation evidence.

中文解释与边界：后期正例7,369，检出488、漏报6,881、误报294、真负例14,878。可以重复运行验证可复现性，但不能据同一test选设计然后称独立检验。

Q32. 为什么validation和test的结果不一样？

Professor may ask: Why do the periods have different results?

English answer: The same fitted model faces different selected cohorts and time periods. Prevalence is 26.19 percent in validation and 32.69 percent in the test. Different performance does not by itself prove that the model improved.

中文解释与边界：模型没重新训练，所以不能把差异说成“后面模型升级了”。正类比例、季节、客源与筛选构成都可能不同，本分析未分离各原因。

Q33. 有没有证明能赚钱或减少取消？

Professor may ask: Did you demonstrate financial or intervention value?

English answer: No. We evaluated prediction and ranking on recorded bookings. We did not run an intervention or measure revenue effects. A review or reminder policy would need a separate evaluation.

中文解释与边界：预测谁会取消，与改变谁会取消是不同研究问题。不要把潜在用途写成实际业务成果。

Q34. 两人怎样参与和分工？

Professor may ask: How did you share the work?

English answer: Both of us participate in every stage, from the research question and data preparation to evaluation, interpretation and presentation. Zhe puts more emphasis on coding and Zhengxuan on slides. We both understand and can explain the complete workflow.

中文解释与边界：每个环节都有两人参与，区别只是投入侧重点。不能理解为Zhe只做代码、Zhengxuan只做PPT；被问到具体任务时按实际工作举例。

Q35. 老师问到暂时不会的问题怎么办？

Professor may ask: What if you cannot answer a detail immediately?

English answer: Let me separate what we verified from what remains uncertain. Our current result supports the selected retrospective cohort. I would need to check the implementation or collect additional information before making a stronger claim.

中文解释与边界：先说已核实的部分，再说明具体未知。可以承认需要查代码或补数据；不要编造参数、原因或结果。这句适合解释证据边界，不能替代学习现有核心方法。

五、术语说明

Booking / Cancellation / No-show：预订／取消／未到店。后三者的最终记录状态决定目标编码。

Noncompletion / Positive / Negative：预订未完成／正例／负例。本项目正例是取消或未到店，负例是完成入住。positive不是“好事”。

Lead time：从录入预订到到店的提前天数；现有记录用于推算日期和输入。

PMS / Change log / Snapshot：酒店管理系统／修改日志／某个时点记录的副本。较晚快照不能自动代表最初取值。

Retrospective / Prospective：回顾性／前瞻性。当前评价过去记录；真实服务需在预测时记录输入，再跟踪未来结果。

Cohort / Eligibility / Prevalence：满足指定条件的一组订单／纳入资格／正类比例。换纳入条件可能换了研究对象。

Estimand：希望估计的具体量。在这里是指定历史人群中模型的预测表现，不是行业因果效应。

Baseline：最低参照模型。这里每笔订单都得到训练正类比例。

Logistic regression / Log-odds：逻辑回归／对数几率。用加权输入描述log[p/(1-p)]，再转成概率。

L2 regularization / C：L2正则化／其反向强度参数。限制权重大小；在相同实现下，较小C通常约束更强。

Log loss / Brier：概率评分。Log loss更强惩罚自信错误；Brier是平均(p-y)^2。数值不能与AUC直接比较大小。

AUC / Average precision (AP)：排序指标。AUC比较正负对；AP按recall增量加权precision。AP受评价集正类比例影响，跨期不能简单当模型变化。

Calibration：校准。给相似概率的订单，实际正例比例是否接近该概率。AUC好不代表概率校准好。

Threshold / Precision / Recall：报警阈值／报警中多少是真的／全部真实正例中找到了多少。

Confusion matrix / TP / FP / FN / TN：混淆矩阵／检出正例／误报／漏报／正确不报警。

Sensitivity analysis：敏感性分析。检查改变处理或评价对象后的结果，不代表解决所有偏差。

Cluster bootstrap / Paired comparison：成组自助重采样／配对比较。按周抽组，同一轮用相同订单评价两个模型。

Dependence / Distribution shift：记录之间有依赖／不同时期的数据构成与关系可能变化。都可能限制评价或外推。

六、读懂三个关键计算

1. Recall：0.5下检出11，总正例3,689。11÷3,689×100%=0.30%。漏掉的3,678仍在分母，因为它们本来就是该找到的病例。

2. Precision：0.5下一共报警35条，其中11真阳性、24误报。11÷35×100%=31.43%。与recall的分母不同。

3. Log loss改善：(0.580026-0.551734)÷0.580026×100%≈4.88%。相对降幅以baseline作为分母；这不是accuracy的变化。
